# Your assistant

Hermes is an assistant that does things for you on a schedule, not just when you
ask. You talk to it in plain language. It reads your mail and calendar, keeps
track of the people and companies you deal with, and can be told to check on
something every morning without you asking again.

## What this is good at

**Things you would otherwise have to remember to do.** Check the calendar every
morning. Sort the inbox. Notice you have not spoken to someone in months.

**Things that take reading a lot to answer.** What has been happening with this
client. Who is this person emailing me. What did we agree last time.

**Drafting, not sending.** It writes; you approve. Anything that leaves — an
email, a message — waits for you unless you have explicitly said otherwise. See
[Reviewing its work](automations/review.md).

## What it is not

It is not a chatbot that forgets you between conversations, and it is not
something that acts behind your back. Every automation is visible on one screen,
and you can switch any of them off there.

It also will not do something it has no access to. If it has not been connected
to your calendar, it cannot read your calendar — it will say so rather than
guess.
