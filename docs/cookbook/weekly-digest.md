# Weekly digest

A Friday summary of what actually happened, and what is still waiting on you.

!!! quote "Say this in chat"

    Every Friday at 4pm, email me a summary of the week: who I spoke to that I
    hadn't before, anything I said I'd do and haven't, and anything that looks
    like it's waiting on me.

**Needs:** mail and calendar. Better with a CRM.

## Why it works

**"Anything I said I'd do and haven't"** is the valuable line, and it is worth
keeping verbatim.

Commitments made in passing — *"I'll send that over"*, *"let me check and come
back to you"* — never become tasks anywhere. They live in a Tuesday email and
then they are gone. This is the only line in the cookbook that catches them, and
it is usually why people keep the digest after dropping other automations.

Friday afternoon is deliberate. Early enough to still act on something, late
enough that the week is done.

## Worth adjusting

**If it reads like a diary**, narrow it: *"just the commitments and what's
waiting on me — skip the summary of what happened"*. Most people end up here
eventually.

**If Friday 4pm is too late**, Friday morning works, at the cost of missing the
day.

**If you want it as a prompt rather than a record**, add "and suggest what I
should prioritise on Monday".

## Safe to auto-send

Addressed to you, seen by nobody else — reasonable to let it send without
approval.
