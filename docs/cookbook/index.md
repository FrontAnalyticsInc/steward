# Cookbook

Recipes you can steal. Each one has the exact wording to paste into
[chat](../chat.md) — adjust the times and names, and the assistant will confirm
before setting anything up.

Each also says what it needs connected. Check the **Integrations** screen if you
are not sure what you have; a recipe that needs your calendar will not work until
the calendar is connected.

## Writing your own

The recipes share a shape, and it is worth copying.

**Say when.** "Every weekday at 7am" beats "each morning" — you get exactly the
schedule you meant.

**Say what to look at.** "The last 30 days of mail" beats "my email". It keeps
the work bounded and the results relevant.

**Say what you want out.** "Email me a list" and "put a draft in my approvals
queue" produce very different automations.

**Say what not to touch.** If it should only report, say "don't change
anything". If it should never send, say "draft only". Left unsaid, it will make
a reasonable guess — but reasonable guesses are how you find out it filed two
hundred emails.

!!! tip "Test it before you trust it"

    After setting one up, open it on the [Automations](../automations/index.md)
    screen and press **Run now**. You find out immediately whether it does what
    you meant, instead of at 7am tomorrow.
