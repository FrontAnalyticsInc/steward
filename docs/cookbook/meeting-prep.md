# Meeting prep

Before anything in your calendar with someone outside the company, get a short
brief on who you are about to meet.

!!! quote "Say this in chat"

    An hour before any meeting with someone outside the company, message me
    what you know about the people attending — who they are, what we last
    discussed, and anything outstanding on their account.

**Needs:** calendar, plus a CRM or its own notes for the context.

## Why it works

It fires when the information is useful. The same facts in a 7am briefing are
forgotten by the time the 3pm call starts — not because the briefing was bad, but
because you read it seven hours and nine interruptions ago.

An hour is the right gap: enough time to look something up if the brief surprises
you, close enough that you still remember it.

## Pair it with a channel

!!! tip

    This is the recipe that most rewards
    [connecting Telegram or Slack](../integrations/channels.md). An hour's warning is only
    useful if it reaches you away from your desk — which, an hour before an
    external meeting, you very often are.

    Delivered to a browser tab you are not looking at, this recipe does nothing.

## Worth adjusting

**"Outside the company" is doing real work here.** Without it you get a brief
before every internal stand-up, which trains you to ignore them.

**If an hour is wrong**, say so — fifteen minutes suits back-to-back days,
a morning-of summary suits a calendar with two meetings in it.

**If you want it only for new people**, say *"only if I haven't met them
before"*. Fewer, more useful briefs.
